Privacy, simply.

Effective November 3, 2026

This policy covers the showmac desktop app and this official website. Contact: 920491071@qq.com.

The app: readings stay local

The current desktop app processes available hardware readings, system state and process activity on your Mac. It has no app account, advertising SDK, third-party analytics or telemetry upload service. showmac does not transmit these readings to us.

History, settings and exports

Recent charts are held in memory for up to 30 minutes and clear when the app quits. Preferences, menu components, selected icons and saved fan curves are stored locally. Widget snapshots are also stored locally. If you export samples, you choose the file location and control any subsequent sharing.

This website

The website has no advertising or third-party analytics. It stores your selected language and appearance in your browser’s local storage. The hosting server keeps standard access and error logs for operation and troubleshooting. These can include IP address, request time, requested path, response status and browser information. Site-specific logs rotate daily and are retained for up to 14 days.

When you contact support

If you email us, we receive the address, message and attachments you choose to send. We use them to handle your request. Email is processed by the email providers involved in delivery. You choose whether to share diagnostic samples or screenshots.

Your choices and updates

You can clear website preferences in your browser, remove local app data, or contact us about support information you have supplied. Changes to data handling will be reflected in this policy and the applicable app release.

Website language

On your first visit, our server looks up your IP address in a local DB-IP country database to choose Chinese for China, Hong Kong, Macao and Taiwan, or English elsewhere. If the country is unknown, browser language is used. This lookup does not send your IP to DB-IP. You can switch languages at any time; your choice is saved in a first-party cookie and local storage for future visits.
