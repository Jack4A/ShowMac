# ShowMac 1.18.11 · build 278

- Add Electricity usage to the sidebar and menu bar More menu. Browse daily, weekly, monthly and yearly recorded kWh, inspect time buckets, and compare earlier recorded periods.
- Set your own flat electricity rate and currency to estimate cost. No default tariff is assumed; changing the rate recalculates historical estimates.
- Integrate nearby valid power observations over time. Record device power, DC input and battery discharge separately, without adding overlapping sources or pretending component power is whole-device power.
- Preserve local hourly history across relaunch, with periodic atomic saves and a flush on normal sleep or quit. Hidden windows continue recording. Sleep, exit, missing readings, duplicate samples and backward-clock overlaps are not filled with invented consumption.
- Show recorded duration, measurement scope and unavailable periods. Internal readings and equivalent battery costs are estimates, not wall-outlet measurements or utility bills. Historical usage before this version cannot be reconstructed.
- Verify integration, source separation, midnight/DST boundaries, restart continuity, persistence, hidden sampling and corrupt-file preservation with isolated fixtures, plus Chinese/English light/dark UI previews.

Release packages target macOS 11+ for Intel and Apple Silicon. Apple Silicon fan control remains experimental and capability-gated. Packages are ad-hoc signed and not notarized. Physical-device validation on macOS 11/12 remains pending. No new hardware-control permissions or telemetry uploads are introduced.
