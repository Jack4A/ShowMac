# ShowMac 1.18.12 · build 279

- Place Today’s electricity in a single Live metrics card: recorded kWh, estimated cost, current watts and a two-minute power trend. Open the whole card for details; the main navigation stays on Live metrics.
- Show measured power curves for daily, weekly, monthly and yearly history. In the day view, choose the full day or an individual hour. Hover or click to read a retained sample’s original timestamp and wattage.
- Persist real first/minimum/maximum/last samples per minute for seven days, then per hour. Keep source separation and visible gaps across missing readings, sleep and restarts. Thinning preserves observed samples rather than replacing them with averaged wattage.
- Preserve existing kWh totals and optional electricity rates. Older energy-only records cannot reconstruct power readings that were never saved. Coverage and cost remain sampling estimates, not wall-meter measurements or utility bills.
- Add checks for real-sample retention, compaction, restart/gap handling, invalid data and corrupt-file preservation. Refresh Chinese/English light/dark previews and usage instructions.

Release packages target macOS 11+ for Intel and Apple Silicon. Apple Silicon fan control remains experimental and capability-gated. Packages are ad-hoc signed and not notarized. Physical-device validation on macOS 11/12 remains pending. No new hardware-control permissions or telemetry uploads are introduced.
