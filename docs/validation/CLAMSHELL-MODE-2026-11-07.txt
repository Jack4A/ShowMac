ShowMac 1.18.23 / 290 — closed-lid mode

Opt-in MacBook-only mode, external-power only. A native authorized helper uses a
fixed pmset invocation and verifies the effective SleepDisabled IOPMrootDomain
property. Direct property writes on newer XNU require an Apple-only entitlement
and are not used. The implementation does not create private entitlements.

Before enabling, a root-owned fixed recovery script and LaunchDaemon are prepared.
The independent guard holds an exclusive root lock, watches the originating app,
parent helper, stop pipe, power source and critical thermal pressure, and retries
restoration until confirmed. Success removes the temporary recovery files. If both
processes are forcibly killed, launchd restores sleep on the next boot. Other
preexisting sleep overrides are rejected. No fan scene or display timer is changed.
