ShowMac 1.18.25 (292) — conditional closed-lid mode, local validation

Behavior
- Two activation choices: manual, or automatically while connected to external power.
- The main switch explicitly arms/disarms automatic operation, including while on battery.
- Displayed states distinguish the saved rule from a verified, currently active sleep override.
- Only an explicitly armed automatic rule resumes after relaunch; manual mode still starts off.
- IOKit power-source notifications recheck eligibility without waiting for the sampling timer.
- Existing helper watchdog independently enforces external power, process lifetime, and critical thermal pressure.
- Disconnection restores sleep while preserving the armed rule; reconnecting can activate a new session.
- A denied authorization or interrupted session pauses retries until explicit retry or a new known power/eligibility interval.
- Unknown power readings do not reset authorization backoff. Other hardware controls defer activation while busy.
- Turning off during a pending activation releases a late successful override.
- Failed replies retain reported ownership so partial activation can be restored.
- Shutdown cancels observation and never restarts the override; existing helper shutdown/boot recovery remains in place.
